RoodNick Channel Digest

RoodNick Channel Digest is a private, personal-use tool developed and operated by RoodNick. It organizes a user's YouTube subscriptions and prepares a categorized daily digest of newly published videos for delivery to the user's Telegram account.

What the application does

  • Reads the authorized user's YouTube subscription list.
  • Reads public channel and video metadata.
  • Organizes channels into user-friendly categories.
  • Creates a daily digest of new videos.

Data handling

The application requests read-only YouTube access. It does not upload videos, modify subscriptions, post comments, or make other changes to the user's YouTube account. Application data and OAuth credentials are stored locally for the authorized user and are not sold or shared with third parties.
